# Privacy Policy — Luxury Finance

**Last updated: 27 October 2026**

Luxury Finance ("the app") is a personal expense tracker published by
Patchanon Poomana. This policy explains what the app does with your
information. The short version: it never leaves your phone.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** The developer receives no data from this app. There is no
account system, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising SDK, and no
server to send anything to.

## What the app stores on your device

Everything you enter stays in the app's private storage on your own phone:

- Your display name, monthly income, and monthly savings target
- The expenses you record: amount, category, description, date, and whether
  you marked the spend as necessary
- Your savings goals and their progress
- Your light/dark theme preference

This data is readable only by the app itself, under Android's per-app storage
sandbox. It is not transmitted anywhere.

## Permissions

The app requests no Android permissions. It does not use the internet,
location, camera, microphone, contacts, or storage outside its own sandbox.

## Deleting your data

Two ways, both immediate and permanent:

- In the app: **ตั้งค่า → ลบข้อมูลทั้งหมด** (Settings → Delete all data)
- Uninstall the app, which removes its private storage

Because the developer never receives your data, there is nothing to request
deletion of from us.

## Backups

If you have Android's system backup enabled, Android may include this app's
data in your own Google account backup. That backup is governed by Google's
privacy policy and your device settings, not by the developer, who has no
access to it. You can turn this off in your device's backup settings.

## Children

The app is a general-purpose finance tool and is not directed at children
under 13. It collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published at this
same URL with a new "last updated" date. Any change that affects what the app
does with your data will also be noted in the app's release notes.
